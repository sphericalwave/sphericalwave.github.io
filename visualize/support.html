Support

Be Still and Know is a personal iOS and macOS app. It is not on the App Store yet.

What it is

Write visualization scripts as an ordered walk through scenes, record each one in your voice, optionally sit a spoken countdown, then play the sequence under a Music-library playlist. Practice sessions, notes, and the Silva 100-to-1 schedule live in the app.

Requirements

  • iPhone or Mac on a current OS
  • iCloud if you want scripts, audio, and images on more than one device

Common stuck points

Other music stops, or you hear nothing. The app is supposed to mix with whatever is already playing. If another app has taken the audio session, stop and play again from Be Still and Know.

No playlists in the countdown picker. Grant Media & Apple Music access in iOS Settings, then reopen the screen. Playlist picking is iPhone-only.

Recordings or pictures missing after a reinstall. They live in SwiftData / CloudKit, not as loose files. Give iCloud a minute to download, then check the Scripts list.

The countdown skipped ahead. “Begin now” ends the vigil and starts the script. Cancel returns you without playing.
