    <h1 class="sw-home__title">Be Still and Know</h1>
    <p class="sw-home__lead">A personal app for visualization practice. Write an ordered walk through scenes, record each one in your own voice, and sit a spoken countdown. A playlist from your Music library can play underneath, if you want it.</p>
    <p class="sw-home__lead">The name is from Psalm 46:10. The practice here is visualization, not reading scripture.</p>

  <div class="sw-home__grid sw-enter-2">

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Scripts</span>
      <h3>Ordered scenes</h3>
      <p>Write the practice as a sequence of scenes. The order is the walk, not a library to browse.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Audio</span>
      <h3>Your own voice</h3>
      <p>Record each scene yourself. The recording stays with that scene. There is no hosted audio service.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Vigil</span>
      <h3>Spoken countdown</h3>
      <p>Sit the countdown before playback. Begin now ends the vigil and starts the script. Cancel returns you without playing.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Music</span>
      <h3>Optional bed</h3>
      <p>On iPhone, a Music-library playlist can play underneath the countdown. The app does not upload that library.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Practice</span>
      <h3>What you keep</h3>
      <p>Sessions, notes, review dates, goal queues, and a Silva 100-to-1 schedule live in the app with the scripts.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Data</span>
      <h3>On your devices</h3>
      <p>Scripts, audio, and images are SwiftData on the device. They sync with CloudKit only if iCloud is on. There is no sphericalWave account.</p>
    </div>
