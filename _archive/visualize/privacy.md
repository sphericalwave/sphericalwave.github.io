    <h1 class="sw-home__title">Privacy</h1>
    <p class="sw-home__lead">Be Still and Know is a personal app for visualization scripts you write and record. This page describes what the app stores and where it goes.</p>
    <p class="sw-home__lead">Last updated August 22, 2026. This policy is published at https://sphericalwave.com/visualize/privacy/.</p>
    <div class="sw-home__cta">
      <a href="/support/?app=visualize" class="sw-home__btn sw-home__btn--primary">Questions</a>
      <a href="/visualize/" class="sw-home__btn sw-home__btn--ghost">Be Still and Know</a>
    </div>
  </section>

  <hr class="sw-home__wave-line">

  <div class="sw-home__grid sw-enter-2">

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">On device</span>
      <h3>What stays on your devices</h3>
      <p>Scripts, scenes, recordings, attached images, practice sessions, review dates, goal queues, and playback settings live in the app's SwiftData store on the device.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">iCloud</span>
      <h3>CloudKit, if it is on</h3>
      <p>If you are signed into iCloud, that store syncs through CloudKit in the container iCloud.com.sphericalwave.beStillAndKnow. Sync uses your Apple ID. Be Still and Know does not run its own account system.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Microphone</span>
      <h3>Recordings</h3>
      <p>Recording a scene uses the microphone. Audio stays in the local store and, if iCloud sync is on, in your CloudKit data. It is not uploaded to a Be Still and Know server — there isn't one.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Photos</span>
      <h3>Attached images</h3>
      <p>You can attach images to a scene from the system photo picker. Those images are stored with the scene, and sync with it if iCloud is on. The app does not browse your whole library.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Apple Music</span>
      <h3>Playlist picker</h3>
      <p>On iPhone, the countdown music bed can read your music library so you can pick a playlist. Be Still and Know does not upload that library.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Diagnostics</span>
      <h3>On-device log</h3>
      <p>An on-device error log records audio-session and CloudKit account-status messages so you can see what happened. It does not leave the device.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Not collected</span>
      <h3>What we do not collect</h3>
      <p>No analytics SDK. No advertising identifier. No crash-reporting service. No Be Still and Know backend that receives your scripts, audio, or images. No push notifications from a server.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Children</span>
      <h3>Not for under 13</h3>
      <p>Be Still and Know is not directed at children under 13.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">Changes</span>
      <h3>If this policy changes</h3>
      <p>If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.</p>
    </div>
