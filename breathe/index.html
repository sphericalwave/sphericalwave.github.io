The four

A tab per practice. A chart for what that practice actually measures.

Open the tab, tap +, run it, save. History is per protocol — mixing a 35-second CO₂ hold into a Wim Hof max would make both numbers useless, so they stay apart.

Breather

O2Trainer work

You breathe against a resistance cap and tap each rep. Thirty reps is the calibration: over 4.5 minutes, drop the cap; under 3 minutes, go higher. The pace chart shades that band.

Basic, Exercise 1, Exercise 2, and Exhale. Cap 1–14 mm. History charts the cap you used.

Breath of Fire

Wim Hof

A round is paced breaths, then an exhale hold you end yourself, then a timed inhale hold. Set breaths per round, cycle length, inhale hold, and how many rounds.

History charts your best hold.

Box Breathing

Square breathing

Four equal sides — inhale, hold, exhale, hold. The interval is 3–15 seconds and applies live, mid-cycle. At 4 seconds the pacer is a square; at 5 it is a pentagon.

History charts duration.

CO₂ Table

CO₂ tolerance

Rest is untimed tidal breathing — no pacer. Then a hold at about 40–50% of a comfortable max. After each hold you rate discomfort 1–10.

Change one thing at a time: shift the rest ladder, or add five seconds to the hold. Not both. History charts rounds completed.

Default table

Rest walks down. The hold stays put.

Eight rounds. Rest from 2:00 to 0:15 in 15-second steps. Hold 35 seconds. You can edit every row.

How you use it

Tab, start, stop, chart.

01

Open the tab

Breather, Breath of Fire, Box, or CO₂ Table. The list is that practice’s history.

02

Tap +

The workout opens in a sheet. Breather counts taps. The others run a pacer.

03

Stop when you are done

Notes are optional. A running workout that leaves the sheet sits in a mini player.

04

Read the chart that belongs

Cap, best hold, duration, or rounds — whichever number that practice actually produces.

On the phone

Cues under the music. Data stays put.

Mixes with other audio

Breath tones, phase voice, and a 15-second hold tick sit on top of whatever is already playing. A boost slider keeps the cue audible without ducking the bed. Box and the CO₂ table can sound at once through one engine.

Box under a CO₂ hold

The CO₂ screen embeds Box Breathing. During the table’s exhale hold, Box pauses itself and comes back on the next inhale, so the two pacers do not fight.

Optional heart rate

A BLE chest strap (Polar H10 and anything else on the standard Heart Rate Service) shows live bpm on the practice screens. An Apple Watch companion can stream HealthKit heart rate. Samples stay in the session on the phone.

iPhone, iOS 17.6+.

Portrait. Optional Apple Watch for heart rate. Sessions, notes, and HR samples live in Core Data on the device — no account, no iCloud, no Breathe server. Not on the App Store yet.
