<p>I'm Aaron — an electrical engineer and mathematician who builds tools.</p>

<p>The degrees came from an obsession, not a career plan: breakthrough energy
technology and physics. I wanted to understand how energy actually moves through
the world, so I studied the math and the electronics that describe it. That
curiosity never closed — a lot of the writing here circles back to it.</p>

<blockquote class="blockquote">
  <p class="mb-2">"If you want to teach people a new way of thinking, don't bother trying to teach them. Instead, give them a tool, the use of which will lead to new ways of thinking."</p>
  <footer class="blockquote-footer mt-1">Buckminster Fuller</footer>
</blockquote>

<p>That line is the thesis behind everything I build. You don't argue people
into changing — you hand them a tool, and the tool does the teaching. So I build
behavior-change apps: software that turns data and dopamine into a feedback loop
so people can become their own coaches. The focus is what I call
<em>optimizing the suit</em> — the body you actually live in — through nutrition
and fitness you can measure and steer yourself.</p>

<p>Jiu-jitsu is where the ideas get pressure-tested. I'm an AJJ black belt
(<em>shodan</em>), and the mats are honest in a way arguments aren't: a theory
either survives resistance or it doesn't. That standard carries back into the
engineering and the writing — if an idea can't take pressure, it isn't finished.</p>

<p>Off the clock, most of my best thinking happens on long walks with my dog,
Alfred.</p>

<p>I'm open to engineering work and contracts — software, hardware, or both.
<a href="mailto:aaron@sphericalwave.com">Get in touch</a>, or see
<a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/work">what I've built</a>.</p>
