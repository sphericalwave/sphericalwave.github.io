  <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__panel sw-enter-2">
    <span class="sw-home__label">The process</span>
    <p class="sw-home__panel-title">Start to finish</p>
    <ol class="sw-steps">
      <li><strong>Design with the client</strong> — Model the patio in CAD and work through the pattern together.</li>
      <li><strong>Order materials</strong> — Pavers, base, and sand, with quantities from the model.</li>
      <li><strong>Prep the site</strong> — Clear and dig out the area.</li>
      <li><strong>Compact and level</strong> — Run the compactor and level the base.</li>
      <li><strong>Brick matrix</strong> — String a grid of lines and lay the bricks to it.</li>
      <li><strong>Cut the walkway</strong> — Saw-cut the pavers to shape the walkway.</li>
      <li><strong>Sand the bricks</strong> — Sweep sand into the joints.</li>
      <li><strong>Topsoil and seed</strong> — Fill in around the edges and seed.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <h2 class="text-center mb-4">Lessons learned</h2>

  <div class="sw-home__grid sw-enter-2">
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">01</span>
      <h3>A concrete saw is necessary</h3>
      <p>You can't cut pavers cleanly without one.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">02</span>
      <h3>CAD is critical</h3>
      <p>The pattern, the cuts, and the material counts all come out of the model.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">03</span>
      <h3>Set up a matrix of lines</h3>
      <p>A grid of string lines gets every stone roughly in the right place, so small errors don't compound across the patio.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">04</span>
      <h3>Bricks are heavy</h3>
      <p>Plan how you'll move them before they arrive.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">05</span>
      <h3>Rent a front end loader</h3>
      <p>Landscaping is much easier with one.</p>
    </div>
