    <span class="sw-home__label">The process</span>
    <p class="sw-home__panel-title">Start to finish</p>
    <ol class="sw-steps">
      <li><strong>Meet with the client</strong> — Walk the site and talk through what they want.</li>
      <li><strong>Initial design</strong> — First version modeled in CAD.</li>
      <li><strong>Iterate the design</strong> — Revise the model with the client until it's right.</li>
      <li><strong>Demolition</strong> — Remove the old concrete steps.</li>
      <li><strong>Post holes</strong> — Dig and set the posts.</li>
      <li><strong>Structure</strong> — Frame the landing.</li>
      <li><strong>Decking</strong> — Lay the cedar deck boards.</li>
      <li><strong>Guard rail</strong> — Rail around the landing.</li>
      <li><strong>Brick work</strong> — Supervise the brick contractor, coordinating the finished level of the landing so the steps fit.</li>
      <li><strong>Stairs</strong> — Cut and set the stringers, then the treads.</li>
      <li><strong>Rail boxes &amp; cladding</strong> — Box the guard rail posts and clad the outside of the steps.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <h2 class="text-center mb-4">Lessons learned</h2>

  <div class="sw-home__grid sw-enter-2">
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">01</span>
      <h3>Rent the post hole digger</h3>
      <p>Digging post holes by hand isn't worth it. Rent the tool.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">02</span>
      <h3>¼" is good enough</h3>
      <p>Precision to a quarter inch is plenty. Chasing tighter than that only costs time.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">03</span>
      <h3>Layout is key</h3>
      <p>Site layout with 12" nails, rope tied off with a constrictor knot, and a line level. Get this right and everything after it lines up.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">04</span>
      <h3>CAD finds the problems first</h3>
      <p>The model surfaces construction challenges before you hit them on site.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">05</span>
      <h3>Drawings pay for themselves</h3>
      <p>Always worth investing in drawings.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">06</span>
      <h3>Laser levels are awesome</h3>
      <p>Worth every penny for setting heights and keeping everything level across the build.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="sw-home__glass sw-home__card">
      <span class="sw-home__label">07</span>
      <h3>The stairs were the hardest part</h3>
      <p>In the end, building the 7-step stringers was the most challenging part of the job.</p>
    </div>
